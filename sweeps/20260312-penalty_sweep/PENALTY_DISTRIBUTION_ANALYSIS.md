## 一、计算管道

```
distance(d) → scale(d) → obj_contrib=(1/evicted_count)×scale → obj_penalty=Σobj_contrib
                                                              → byte_penalty=Σbyte_contrib
             ↓
penalty = w_obj × obj_penalty + w_byte × byte_penalty
             ↓
reward_preclip = formula(penalty, good_rate)
             ↓
final_reward = clip(reward_preclip, -1, 1)
```
